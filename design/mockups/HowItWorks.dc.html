How it works

One order, followed from purchase order to doorstep.

COAST sits alongside your ERP and follows every order through each handoff: you to supplier, supplier to warehouse or straight to your customer, and carrier to doorstep. Here's exactly what happens at each step, what runs automatically, and where a person stays in control.

1 · Order & PO 2 · Supplier response 3 · On the move 4 · Delivered & learning

It starts with how each line is fulfilled

Every order line is tagged with how it will reach the customer. COAST uses this to pick the right stages to track, so a stock item isn't left waiting for a supplier that doesn't exist.

From stock
Already on your shelves. COAST tracks it from dispatch to delivery.
Picked → Dispatched → In transit → Delivered
Via your warehouse
Bought in, received by you, then sent on. COAST tracks both legs of the journey.
PO → Confirmed → Inbound → Received → Dispatched → Delivered
Direct from supplier
The supplier ships straight to your customer, with the supplier's identity masked throughout.
PO → Confirmed → Dispatched → In transit → Delivered

How COAST decides

Automatic when it's sure. Your call when it isn't.

Every piece of information records where it came from and how confident COAST is in it. The rules are fixed and traceable, so you can always see why something happened.

● High confidence → applied automatically
  • Supplier confirms through the magic link
  • Email matches a known supplier template
  • A tracking number is detected
  • A single, clear delivery date
● Medium or low → sent to the review queue
  • Vague wording like "ASAP" or "end of month"
  • A date range rather than a date
  • Conflicting details in one email
  • Nothing changes until someone accepts, edits or rejects
