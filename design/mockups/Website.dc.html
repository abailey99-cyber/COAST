How it works

From PO to doorstep, on its own.

  1. 01
    PO goes out automatically
    The supplier gets the PO with a no-login link to confirm their delivery date from their phone.
  2. 02
    Replies are read for you
    Confirmations and dispatch emails are matched to the right PO. Anything uncertain goes to a review queue.
  3. 03
    Carriers tracked live
    Tracking numbers become live shipments across DHL, FedEx, UPS, DPD, Royal Mail and more.
  4. 04
    Customers see the truth
    A self-updating delivery date and plain-English status, with supplier details masked at the data layer.
